## Product boundaries

- Email is demo/preview-only; no verified FitBox sender or background scheduler is configured.
- Notification control currently affects in-app coach notifications. OS push/always-on Messenger-style overlays are not claimed.
- Phone call-state handling requires optional READ_PHONE_STATE permission. The listener consumes status only, never phone numbers or call logs. Focus/background pause remains the default fallback. Video-call overlays depend on focus/background behavior; no call-specific API or microphone permission is used.
- External music apps are not controlled by FitBox. No exclusive music focus or microphone capture is requested. Physical-device Spotify/Apple Music/YouTube Music combinations and battery consumption are not certified.
- Store gear is virtual and bought with earned SP. It is device-local; it is not physical merchandise fulfillment or real-money cosmetic checkout.
- Exclusions prevent selected movements; the app cannot diagnose medical conditions or prescribe safe substitutions. Muscle highlights show exercise associations, not hypertrophy/fat/atrophy measurements.
- Historical training-calendar statuses are reconstructed using current preferences. The current day uses a deterministic rotation; profile changes may change its task list. There is no immutable server-issued workout prescription yet.
- A camera clip is limited to two minutes and 50 MB, stops on interruption, and stays in the app's private local storage. Added media requires explicit selection and exercise assignment; arbitrary system-gallery content is not scanned or classified.
- Physical FitBox hardware pairing requires firmware service UUIDs/protocol. Opening native Bluetooth settings does not invent hardware integration.
