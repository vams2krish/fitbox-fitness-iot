## Deliberate boundaries

Email remains demo-only by explicit user choice. No Resend sender/domain/API key or unrelated product domain is connected. No real report is sent. Account report email is contact data, not proof of a verified mailbox.

Coach bubbles are inside FitBox; no permission to draw over other apps or OS notification service is installed. Daily report preparation occurs on app open/day end; it does not pretend to be a background email scheduler.

The 3D body is a procedural reference mannequin, not the missing original high-fidelity 3D anatomy asset or a reconstruction of the user. Pose tracking is on-device and remains experimental; no physical-phone accuracy, body-composition, muscle-growth or calorie claim is made.

Goals, reveals, favorites, reviews and coach/report state stay in local storage and are included in device export. Consent-gated cloud sync covers workout records/claims and selected profile fields, including activity preferences and report email. Scores are user-controlled practice records, not verified competition results.

Exact source artwork, language translations and unreviewed Figma variants remain in the recovery audit. The Figma high-fidelity tool still reports a Starter-plan quota limit. These gaps are not silently counted as complete.
