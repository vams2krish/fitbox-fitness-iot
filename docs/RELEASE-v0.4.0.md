# FitBox v0.4.0 — Studio fitness & on-device AI

A fitness-first visual revision inspired by the Figma flow: digital athlete, computer-vision styling, XP/levels, mystery sessions and unlockable virtual gear. Supplied artwork is excluded from this app build.

- Cumulative saved-today plus current-set progress, dynamic ready/active/paused/complete rings, paused draft recovery and no double-saving.
- Claimed workouts hidden by default, with an eye toggle; fresh rotating daily plans, no missed-workout debt and movement exclusions.
- Interactive insights and worklogs, daily/weekly/monthly/yearly analytics, HTML/text email templates, CSV/JSON and Print/Save PDF. Email remains explicitly unsent.
- Private exercise-tagged local image/video gallery and optional silent recording. No media uploads or whole-gallery scanning.
- Blender-adapted detailed digital athlete with muscle highlights and virtual outfit/cap/bottle cosmetics. CC BY-SA credits and sources included; female variant is illustrative.
- SP wallet, cosmetics and level rewards, persistent settings, voice cues and break reminders, optional call-state protection, Bluetooth settings, support email/screenshots and confirmed cloud-account deletion.

See README and docs/V0.4-STATUS.md for verification and remaining product boundaries. Calls never auto-resume workouts. External music depends on its provider's background-playback support. Battery savings are implementation choices, not measured device battery-life guarantees.

Web: https://fitbox-fitness-iot.vercel.app
