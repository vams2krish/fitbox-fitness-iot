# FitBox coach reminders

FitBox 0.6.0 implements opt-in **scheduled Android system notifications**. The web app provides schedule previews; it does not implement remote Web Push or FCM. No paid messaging service is required.

## Timing and tone

Workout invitations aim for 30 or 60 minutes before the chosen workout time. Sleep hours and optional work hours are quiet periods: reminders landing there are skipped, not forced through. Recovery messages use an available daytime/evening slot. The shared daily limit is two reminders by default, optionally one. All categories respect quiet hours at scheduling and delivery.

Copy uses the selected coach, first name, experience and recent FitBox logs. It offers an easy return without guilt, assumes nothing about exercise outside FitBox, and never diagnoses health or threatens lost progress. Membership messages require a recently verified cancellation/end date; cached status is not a real-time billing feed while the app is closed.

Time zone follows the device, including changes. No GPS or location permission is used. Reminders are local to this device, with a 14-day plan refreshed when the app opens or preferences/progress change. A gentle comeback reminder can recur weekly after inactivity. Timezone/boot/package-update receivers restore future alarms. Force-stopping the app can prevent delivery until it is opened again.

## Android delivery

Enable through **Settings → Coach reminders**. Android 13+ permission is requested only after the user chooses Enable. Separate workout, recovery and membership channels have sound and silent variants. Android channel preferences and Do Not Disturb take priority. Notification content is private on the lock screen; tapping opens the relevant FitBox section.

[AlarmManager inexact alarms](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms) avoid requesting exact-alarm privileges. Battery restrictions and idle mode can delay delivery. Stale workout messages expire instead of appearing hours late. No continuous background camera, location service or polling is required.

Three short original chimes are generated by `tools/generate-nudge-sounds.py`; source and WAV files are included under [CC0](NUDGE-SOUND-LICENSE.txt), permitting commercial reuse. Category icons and existing pixel coach imagery match the app. This release has emulator checks; physical-device sound loudness and manufacturer-specific battery behavior still need device testing.

An optional follow-up is scheduled five minutes after workout time. Opening FitBox after the advance reminder or starting any activity that day suppresses it. There is at most one follow-up; it also respects quiet hours and the daily cap.
